About

This demo is built by scripts/demo-build.php from the package itself: the manifest, the service worker, the client and the Blade components are the real ones, rendered with the demo's configuration.

Installed app

In the installed app on a desktop, the Back and Reload buttons appear in the header (<x-pwa::nav/>), because the browser does not show its own in standalone mode.
